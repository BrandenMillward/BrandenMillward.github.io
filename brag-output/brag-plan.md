# Brag Plan: brandenmillward.com

## What is this app?
The personal site of Branden Millward, AI Orchestration Architect: a portfolio, blog and
thirteen project case studies, built around an interactive nine-layer diagram of the agent
architecture he designs, where selecting any layer opens real evidence from his work.

## The angle
The site's whole argument is one sentence: "Most organisations don't have an AI problem.
They have a trust problem." Everything else on the page exists to prove it. So the video is
that argument, built in front of you: the nine-layer reference architecture assembles layer
by layer, the accountability layer arrives in a different colour on purpose, and then a real
click opens the evidence behind it. Not a portfolio tour. The thesis, assembled.

This is specific to this project because the diagram is the product. No other portfolio has
these nine layers, this ochre-means-a-human-is-answerable rule, or this claim.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Black ground. One line in Archivo, full scale:

    Most organisations don't have an AI problem.

Hold. Then the second line lands, with "trust" in accent blue:

    They have a trust problem.

His own lede, unedited. It earns the next 18 seconds because it is a claim, not a greeting.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The nine-layer architecture assembling top to bottom, in five grouped reveals: the four
  capability cards (RETRIEVAL / MEMORY / TOOLS / SKILLS), then wires converging on
  AGENTS · ROLES & HANDOFFS, then ORCHESTRATOR · PROTOCOLS, then the control pair
  GUARDRAILS + GOVERNANCE arriving in ochre, then OUTPUT · MEASURED.
- The ochre beat. On the real site, ochre is never decorative: it means a human stays
  answerable for what happens there. The two control nodes land in a different colour from
  everything above them, and that difference is the point of the whole system.
- A real click. The cursor selects GOVERNANCE, the node goes active, and the live detail
  panel below resolves to its actual copy: "The barrier to adoption is auditability, not
  capability." That is the site's strongest line and it is only reachable by using the thing.

## Outro / punchline
The diagram recedes. The site's h1 at full scale, then the domain:

    I turn AI strategy into deployed systems.
    brandenmillward.com

## User flow worth showing
Yes, the site has a real interaction, and it is the centrepiece.
entry -> the nine-layer diagram, at rest, showing "Nine layers, one accountable system"
key action -> select a layer (GOVERNANCE)
result -> the node goes active and the live region swaps to that layer's real evidence

Scene 3 is that flow, simulated exactly: cursor, selection state, panel swap.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: an architect's reference drawing assembling itself, quiet and certain
- Interpretation: four scenes, long holds, soft crossfades, no aggressive motion. The
  restraint is the argument: a video about accountability should not shout. Nothing flashes,
  nothing spins, every line sits long enough to be read twice.

## Audio direction
- Role: sparse professional accents over a low music bed
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app.mp3 (109.96 BPM, 60s, the calmest
  bundled track and the right tempo for restraint)
- Music treatment: start at 0.0s, sit low under the hook, lift slightly as the architecture
  assembles, carry the outro on the 20.19s strong cue, fade out by 21.2s. Never louder than
  the type.
- Music cue guidance: preset cue file read (assets/music/cues/...vol-10...music-cues.json).
  Beat grid ~0.545s spacing. Strong cue at 20.19s: target the outro settle there. Use every
  other beat (~1.09s) for the grouped architecture reveals in Scene 2 and nothing faster,
  because these are text labels and must clear the reading floor.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle. The diagram's wire glow and node presence may breathe
  with music energy. No waveform bars, no pulsing on the type.
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched, professional restraint. Roughly five cues in the whole
  video: one soft tick per grouped node reveal, one distinctly warmer accent on the ochre
  control pair, one real UI click on the cursor selection.
- Audio-coupled moments: the five grouped node reveals in Scene 2; the ochre arrival; the
  cursor click and panel swap in Scene 3.
- Restraint rule: no whooshes, no risers, no impact hits, no sound on the hook or the outro
  type. Silence is allowed to carry the last beat. If a cue is not matching a real visual
  movement, it does not belong.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The claim — 4.5s (0.0-4.5)
Ground #0a0c16, empty. Line one, Archivo ~64px, #e6e9f5, centred on an editorial measure:
"Most organisations don't have an AI problem." Fast-in (0.4s), settles, holds ~1.8s. Line two
arrives below it: "They have a trust problem." with the word "trust" in #8b9dff. Holds ~1.6s.
Both lines on screen together at the end of the scene. No diagram yet, no name, no chrome.
Sequential/interaction: yes — two lines, second lands about 2.2s after the first, each held
past its reading floor (7 words and 5 words).
Audio intent: near silence with the bed barely present. The claim should feel stated, not sold.
Audio-coupled idea: none. No sound on the hook type.
Music: low bed, under the type.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The architecture assembles — 7.0s (4.5-11.5)
The nine-layer diagram builds on the same dark ground, centred, recreated from the site's
.net-svg: 4px-radius rects, 1px lines, JetBrains Mono uppercase labels. Five grouped reveals
spaced ~1.09s (every other beat):
  1. Four capability cards in a 2x2: RETRIEVAL, MEMORY, TOOLS, SKILLS
  2. Wires converge; AGENTS · ROLES & HANDOFFS lands full width
  3. ORCHESTRATOR · PROTOCOLS lands full width
  4. GUARDRAILS and GOVERNANCE land as a pair, in #e0a458, not the accent blue
  5. OUTPUT · MEASURED lands full width
Each node fades and settles in ~0.35s, no sliding, no bounce. A small mono caption sits above
the drawing throughout: "How agent networks fit together". After the fifth reveal, the
complete diagram holds ~1.4s.
Sequential/interaction: yes — five grouped reveals, in flow order, top to bottom.
Audio intent: quiet accumulation. Each group arriving should feel like a part seating into
place, not a notification.
Audio-coupled idea: one soft tick per grouped reveal, beat-aligned; the ochre pair gets a
warmer, slightly fuller cue than the other four to mark that it means something different.
Music: bed lifts slightly as the stack accumulates.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Select a layer — 5.5s (11.5-17.0)
The assembled diagram stays on screen, now with the real detail panel beneath it showing its
rest state in mono: "Nine layers, one accountable system". A cursor moves to the GOVERNANCE
node and clicks (~1.0s in). The node takes its active state in #e0a458 with an
--accountable-soft fill. The panel below swaps, live-region style, to:

    GOVERNANCE: ACCOUNTABILITY
    The barrier to adoption is auditability, not capability.

The label in mono uppercase #e0a458, the line in Newsreader ~34px #e6e9f5. The swapped panel
holds ~2.8s, well past the reading floor for a nine-word sentence.
Sequential/interaction: yes — simulate the real click: cursor travel, selection state on the
node, then the panel content swap. This is the site's actual interaction, not a mockup.
Audio intent: one honest UI click and then room. The line should land in near silence.
Audio-coupled idea: a single dry click at the cursor down, a very soft cue on the panel swap.
Nothing on the sentence itself.
Music: bed holds steady, no build.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — The line — 4.2s (17.0-21.2)
The diagram fades back and down to a faint trace. The site's h1 arrives at full scale, Archivo
~72px, #e6e9f5: "I turn AI strategy into deployed systems." Holds ~2.2s. Then a mono line
settles beneath it in #8b9dff: "brandenmillward.com", landing on the 20.19s strong cue. Both
hold to the end. Final frame is the name and the domain on the dark ground, with the faint
architecture still visible behind.
Sequential/interaction: yes — headline first, domain ~2.4s later on the strong cue.
Audio intent: the bed carries the strong cue and fades. The last half second is quiet.
Audio-coupled idea: none. No sound on the outro type, no logo hit.
Music: fade out by 21.2s.
Transition mood: end

**Music mood for this video:** calm, low, professional; upbeat only in the sense that it moves
**Audio summary:** A quiet bed that barely exists under the claim, accumulates five small
motion-matched ticks as the architecture seats into place, gives one real click to the
interaction, and carries the closing line on a single strong cue before fading to silence.
